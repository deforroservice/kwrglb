What we do

Connecting research, funding and international collaboration

KWARG works at the intersection of scientific research, technology and international development. We help organisations translate their expertise and ambitions into credible collaborative projects, combining technical understanding with structured project development and practical coordination.

About us →

  • Funding Intelligence

    Identifying and assessing international research and innovation funding opportunities based on programme requirements, scientific relevance, institutional priorities and technical feasibility.

  • Project & Proposal Development

    Transforming research concepts into competitive proposals through structured methodologies, work packages, implementation plans, budgets and coordinated partner contributions.

  • Partnerships & Consortia

    Building international collaborations between universities, research institutes, NGOs, public institutions and technology companies around complementary expertise and shared objectives.
