Partnership

International Technology Partnerships for Social Impact

With Tech To The Rescue, building a portfolio of AI, data and geospatial projects for nonprofits across Europe, Africa, Asia and Latin America.

Contact the team →All projects
Strategic partner
Tech To The Rescue
Geographic scope
Europe, Africa, Asia, Latin America
Focus
AI & data intelligence, climate & sustainability, humanitarian technology, digital transformation, applied R&D

Tech To The Rescue is an international nonprofit connecting technology companies, research expertise and social-impact organisations. Together we helped nonprofits tackling urgent social challenges turn real-world problems into actionable technology projects, connecting them with the right expertise, partners and development resources.

The challenge

Nonprofits often have deep domain expertise, established networks and clear missions, but lack access to the specialised technology needed to modernise operations, unlock the value of their data or build scalable digital solutions.

The partnership brought KWARG's capabilities in AI, data engineering, geospatial intelligence and applied R&D together with Tech To The Rescue's international network. KWARG was one of the leading technology partners, identifying opportunities, coordinating stakeholders, evaluating technical requirements and developing project concepts.

Objectives

  • Technology accessConnect social-impact organisations with expertise in AI, data analytics, geospatial technology and digital product development.
  • Project qualificationAssess opportunities by strategic relevance, technical feasibility, resources needed and implementation value.
  • Cross-sector collaborationBuild working relationships between nonprofits, technology professionals and academic institutions.
  • Solution pathwaysTurn organisational challenges into actionable technical scopes and implementation approaches.
  • Capacity developmentExplore delivery models that make advanced technology expertise accessible to organisations with limited technical capacity.

KWARG contributions

  • Expertise on satellite observations, geospatial analytics and environmental intelligence for a humanitarian digital platform supporting operational decisions in high environmental-stress regions.
  • Support for a South African social-impact organisation, assessing an emotionally responsive AI system to help address gender-based violence in schools.
  • AI-assisted sustainability planning, gamified data collection and community engagement tools for an organisation in South America.
  • Consulting on financial reporting platforms for organisations combating human trafficking, dashboards for social-support helplines and analytics for service coordination.
  • A strategic collaboration with HU University of Applied Sciences Utrecht on a three-party delivery model linking nonprofits, KWARG’s technical advisory and project management, and university-based applied R&D.

Results

The partnership produced a portfolio of technology engagements spanning AI systems, environmental intelligence, satellite data, digital service platforms, organisational analytics and sustainability applications. Through structured project selection and consultations, KWARG took opportunities from first identification through technical discovery to digital implementation.

At its core, the partnership helped resource-constrained nonprofits solve real operational challenges, access expertise they could not otherwise afford, and build the digital capabilities to deliver greater impact.
